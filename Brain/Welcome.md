# Welcome to Scepter 🗡️

This is your second brain — the part of your mind that survives device
swaps, wipes, and upgrades.

## Quick links

- [[Index of Conversations]] — every session with Hermes, newest first
- Hermes memory: `Hermes/Memory/`
- Your notes start in `Brain/` — create freely, link with `[[wikilinks]]`

## How it works

1. **Hermes writes memory directly here** — `~/.hermes/memories` is a symlink into this vault.
2. **Sessions export here automatically** — a scheduled job turns the local
   conversation DB into readable Markdown.
3. **Git keeps it everywhere** — push from Termux, pull on your phone/PC.

## Daily practice (optional)

- Jot one thing learned today in `Brain/Journal/`
- Link it to a session note: `See [[2026-08-06_scepter-brainstorm]]`

The vault is yours — Hermes just lives in the `Hermes/` corner of it.
